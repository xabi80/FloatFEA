## What it changes, for a reader who finds this file first

**FS0 reverses the priority the last four commits ran on.** Step 3 had spent three
commits on the two blocking items step 2 carried (R845, R846) and then on repairs to
the gate those produced (R850, R851, R853, R857–R860) — every one of them real, every
one of them in the gating apparatus or in prose about it, and none of them FL2. FS0
stops that: **apparatus, report guards and deliverable prose wait until FL2's five
items exist**, with the single exception of a finding that blocks under FR1.

The five items are given in an order, which they did not have before. It runs from the
model outward — topology, then what is driven through it (headings, the band check),
then what re-solves it (the two sizing analyses) — so each item stands on the one
before it.

**R862 is placed rather than ledgered.** `floatfea/run/pipeline.py:673` computes the
published `| total |` from a hand-copied four-stage list, and
`tests/regression/test_r1_example_golden.py:318`/`:321` carry two more copies. Today
every copy agrees and the published total is correct. The commit that adds a timed
stage is the commit where they stop agreeing — a measured 40.000 s short, with the test
whose own message reads "total must be the sum of the stages" passing, because it uses
the same hand copy. FS0 puts the repair in that commit, which is the first of items 4
and 5 to time itself.

**FS1 and FS2 are the two FF0 items the loop could not absorb**, and each gets one
standalone `process:` commit. FS1 retires the EG3 enumerations in favour of the
predicate the reviewer verified ten times: *every red names a finding or a site of the
newest verdict; any other red blocks.* The lists were corrected at EH1, EJ2, FN2, in a
step-2 report, and in verdicts R835 and R861 — five adoptions and two withdrawals — and
the predicate was right at every application. FS2 gives `scripts/write_verdict.py` an
`--interim` mode, because FR3 plus ES0 make "an open step with no report" the normal
state of an interim check and the tool refused it three times, so the last three
verdicts were hand-written.

**FS3 sends R852 to FL3 rather than fixing it here.** A caveat truncated to its own
marker passes every check once the artifacts regenerate, because both halves then carry
the stub and no code-to-file comparison can see it. The answer is a committed record of
the nine texts that the generator reads and the guide cites — which belongs with the
guide, in step 4.

**FS4 is a checkpoint, not a new deadline.** The target stays 5 November (FR0). What is
new is a dated reporting obligation: if FL2 is not in its first reviewed revision by
26 October, that is reported on 26 October with the remaining items and a realistic
date, rather than at the step's close.
