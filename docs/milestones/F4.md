## 2. STEP 1 — LOAD MAPPING

### 2.0 The premise check comes first (EK0(a))

Before anything is mapped: over all six cases, the per-body external hydrodynamic force
on each of the five FE bodies is read from the solve and must be **identically zero**.
**Report the max. If it is nonzero, STOP** — the superstructure-only scope is the premise
of everything below it, and a nonzero value means the five bodies are not dry.

### 2.1 What it builds

1. **The reader and the mapper.** Each v1 record's load reaches the FE nodes of the body
   its label names, by physical origin, preserving total force and moment about a common
   point (G4.4).
2. **The joint reactions (EK0(e)).** `res.lam` at the 12 buoy–hub joints and the 4
   hub–platform joints — three forces and the locked-axis moment — applied at
   `buoy_joint_nodes` and the four hub-to-platform nodes. **Equal and opposite on the two
   bodies of each joint, asserted.**
3. **The static case (EK0(d)).** Self-weight from the FE mass, the DY0 distribution, per
   body, carried at its joints. Supports are **vertical at every joint and horizontally
   free** — the buoys float, so the joints restrain no horizontal motion. Horizontal and
   yaw rigid motion is removed by a minimal statically determinate restraint whose
   **reactions must be zero**. Hubs: own weight plus the platform's joint reaction on 3
   buoy joints, determinate. Platform: 4 hub joints, the single redundancy resolved by
   the FE stiffness, with **the four reactions asserted equal by symmetry**. Report the
   reactions and `Σ = weight` per body.
4. **The additive HSP writer** (`scripts/`, outside the gate, started under EJ4). It
   carries `res.lam`, the per-body external force, and `mu[N,6]` — already a required v1
   channel at `docs/load-interchange-v1.md:87`. **It does not carry an equilibrium
   reaction**, because that reaction is zero.

*Item 4 of the draft — buoyancy from the hull geometry on the mean wetted surface — is
removed by EK0(b) and recorded in § 8.*

## 3. STEP 2 — SOLVE: INERTIA RELIEF PER BODY

Inertia relief via Lagrange multipliers on six rigid-body constraints per body, which has
the property `PLAN.md:305-307` names: the multipliers **are** the residual reactions and
double as a diagnostic.

* **G4.1 (DQ8)** — per body and per case, never aggregated, and **the static and dynamic
  parts are never mixed**. Dynamic: the 6-component discrete residual of
  `Σ joint reactions − M·a` on FloatSim's accelerations, normalised by that body's
  `max |Σ reactions|` over the window, **plus the assertion that the FE inertia-relief
  acceleration equals FloatSim's**. Static: `|Σ reactions − weight| / weight`.
* **DQ4** — the `M·a` test on the nodal force **vector**, (i) per body against the deck's
  `M·a` and `J_G·α`, (ii) per element against the closed form.
* **DQ5** — V4.2 / G4.2 driven by an independent body force built from DY0's split by
  closed-form consistent loads, **never through `M`**: `a = g`, and every member force
  and moment `≈ 0`, normalised by `μ·g·L` and `μ·g·L²`.
* **G4.4** — mapping conservation: total force and moment about a common point preserved
  per body and per source.
* **G4.5 is a MEASUREMENT, not a gate (EK0(c))** — one case at T = 14 s, at `dt` and
  `dt/2`, with the residual rate reported in the closure artifact.

**The residual form is the discrete one, and that decision is already taken.** The
continuous identity does not close to round-off: `newmark.py:48` documents
`mu_{n+1-alpha_f} ~= mu_n` as an O(h) lag, and `docs/load-interchange-v1.md` § 4.1-4.2
chose the discrete form so that G4.1 means what it says.
`scripts/report_joint_reactions.py::discrete_residual` forms it today.
