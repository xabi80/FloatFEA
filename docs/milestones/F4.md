## 7a. HOW F4 REPORTS

Reports follow **EI4, minimum form**: what the guards require, plus measurements and
findings. No narrative, and no figure restated outside a generated block. The one
hand-written paragraph carries the schedule — which date the step is measured against,
and whether it holds.

Three verdicts per step, and a plan re-lock inside a step does not restart that count
(DK0). **EK3:** a prose defect found after a step closes is fixed in the next step's
first report or in `docs/closure/**` alone, and does not open a round.

**Push ordering is standing practice:** a report revision is pushed as the HEAD of a push
whose union also carries a file CI runs on — `docs/closure/**`, `docs/milestones/**` and
`tests/corpus/**` all qualify. Verdict 90 derived it and verdict 92 confirmed it.

## 8. WHAT F4 DOES NOT DO

**Removed by EK0(b) — not applicable to the superstructure; deferred with the buoy spar
columns:**

* **Buoyancy from hull geometry** on the mean wetted surface. The five FE bodies have no
  wetted surface.
* **G4.3** — the pure hydrostatic case reproducing an analytically known buoyancy
  distribution.
* **G4.6** — hydrostatic reconciliation of recomputed buoyancy against FloatSim's `C`,
  per body, 6×6.
* **Strip-resolved distributed loads.** No record mapped in F4 carries a distributed
  hydrodynamic field, because no FE body in F4 is wetted. *The non-negotiable is
  unchanged: if such a record ever arrives without strip resolution it is refused, not
  distributed by assumption.*

**Deferred to F6 by EX4, renumbered by EZ3:**

* **The FE-side acceleration agreement.** DQ8 asked, beside the two residuals, that the FE
  inertia-relief acceleration equal FloatSim's per body, normalised by `max_t |a|` and
  `max_t |α|`. FloatSim's side is `res.xi_ddot` and is in hand; the FE side needs the
  dynamic loads mapped onto members at each step. **No tolerance is declared for it in F4**
  — EA4 forbids declaring one before the measurement exists, and F4's table has no row for
  it. The two residual channels EV1 gates are unaffected and stay in F4.

**Also not done:**

* It does not add a `gravity` or `hydrostatic` channel to the interchange schema. Both
  are § 7 decisions and `docs/load-interchange-v1.md:670` says what adding them would
  look like.
* It does not modify FloatSim's solve path. The HSP writer is additive, in `scripts/`,
  and inert when output is disabled.
* It does not give the buoys FE mass (DQ7).
* It does not open new apparatus (DR1 stands). **EK2 permits repairing a locator or
  parser that misreads its input; a wrong assertion is deleted, not repaired.**
* It does not solve the shell sub-models. Those are exported to CalculiX.
