## 1. DJ0 — THE JOINT MODEL (verbatim)

> A two-rotation gimbal is modelled as a release of the two free moments with the
> locked-axis moment transmitted.

and

> all 16 joints are two-rotation gimbals

**The second is a claim to VERIFY, not a fact to assume.** DL3 puts that
verification in F5-prep — "DJ0's verification that all 16 joints are
two-rotation gimbals" — so F3 reads it as an input and F5-prep checks it against
the platform definition. If a joint turns out not to be a gimbal, F3's builder
refuses the model rather than idealising it.

**Already built and tested**, in F2 step 7:

| what | where |
|---|---|
| the release pattern | `floatfea/element/releases.py`, `gimbal_release(end, locked_axis)` |
| two released and the third transmitted, both halves asserted | `tests/verification/rung2/test_releases_and_links.py` |
| the rigid-link kinematic transfer the joint offsets need | `floatfea/element/constraints.py` |

So F3's joint work is *application*, not formulation: mapping each of the 16
joints onto a locked axis and an offset, and asserting the mapping rather than the
algebra.

## 2. BUILDER LIMITS (verbatim)

```
L/D >= 2
L/r <= 300
```

**The builder REFUSES a member outside either limit.** Not a warning, not a
clamp: `CLAUDE.md` § Non-negotiables. `L/D >= 2` keeps the member in the range a
beam element describes at all — below it the cross-section is not slender against
the span and a space frame is the wrong model. `L/r <= 300` is the slenderness
ceiling Xabier set.

**Both limits are a refusal and neither is a tolerance.** They are properties of
the model the tool accepts, declared in the plan and asserted by the builder, and
they do not belong in `floatfea/tolerances.py` — nothing is compared against them
to decide whether a computed number is close enough to another.

## 5. GATES

| gate | what it proves | ladder |
|---|---|---|
| **G3.1a** | Per-body mass, CoG and inertia tensor from the FE mesh against the model definition. **Reported and asserted PER BODY, never on the sum** — a global total that matches while individual bodies do not is a failure | V3.1a |
| **G3.1b** | The same properties against the FloatSim body definitions | V3.1b |
| **G2.1 ON REAL MEMBERS (R486, DD0, DI1)** | G2.1's element-local rigid residual and its seventh-mode bound asserted on **every member of the real platform model at its real orientation and proportions**, and **the builder refuses a platform that fails it** | — |

### R486's gate, made concrete

**What it replaces.** R486 asked for the rigid-mode ceiling shown valid across
the whole *admissible* domain. DD0 moved it out of F2: "every admissible
configuration" is an open-ended domain and each round found one more axis of it.
Asserting the ceiling on the platform's own members is a **stronger statement
about the structure being analysed and a weaker one about structures in general**,
and the trade is deliberate.

**What it asserts, per member:**

```
element_rigid_residual(k_local, L) <= RIGID_MODE_EXACTNESS
```

using the element-local form F2 step 6 shipped as a diagnostic — six rigid
motions about the element's own midpoint, homogenisation on the matrix, so
orientation, span and reference point leave the quantity by construction.

**And its three counters go back into the injection guard.** F2 step 6 took the
residual pair out of `tests/test_counters_are_injected.py` because the gate it
defended had become a diagnostic; here it is an assertion again, so
`dropped_flip`, `wrong_dof_index` and `rotational_block` are registered against
it. `scripts/rigid_counter_response.py` measures what each reddens, and **the
count of elements a counter fails to reach is a number to take at the commit, not
to carry forward** — F2 saw that figure move three times with no code change.

**What F3 must check that F2 could not.** The near-vertical band. R486's original
finding was a defect-free element reading `2.9994e-14` against `1.068e-16` at
2.87° from vertical on a *retired assembled* form. The element-local form reads
0.007–0.035 eps in that band. A platform frame is mostly near-vertical members,
so this gate is measured there first.

## 6. WHAT F3 DOES NOT DO

* **The band on G2.4.** F2 rewrote AO4 (DL0) and left the convergence-order
  window measured rather than asserted, because a lower bound's counter widens
  downward and `tests/test_counters_are_injected.py` computes every widened
  ceiling as `WIDEN * ceiling`. That is an apparatus change and CZ0 freezes
  apparatus through F6.
* **V1.3, V2.3, V2.4.** Deferred in F2 with reasons in
  `docs/closure/F2-step6.md` §6. F3 reads none of them.
* **Frequencies in the golden.** `tests/regression/test_f2_shipped_matrices.py`
  records matrices and not frequencies, because the cross-platform drift of an
  `eigh` result is unmeasured. Taking that measurement is one CI run and it is
  F3's to take if F3 wants frequencies protected.
