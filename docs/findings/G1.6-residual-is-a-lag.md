# The G1.6 radiation residual is dominantly a COHERENT LAG, not a missing term

**From:** FloatFEA, AD1–AD3 + the X3 guard, 2026-08-25
**Status:** measured. **The mechanism is not yet identified**, and the leading
suspect is FloatFEA's own comparison harness — see §4.

---

## 1. AD1 and AD3 both came back against expectation

**AD1 — the bridge premise HOLDS on this record.** The general argument is
correct: `arg(ΣA_jk X_k) = arg(ΣB_jk X_k)` is guaranteed only for an uncoupled
DOF, and with 96.8% off-diagonal energy there is no reason it should survive.
Measured on the actual response, it survives anyway:

```
DOF     |A-term|     |B-term|    arg(A)-arg(B)
 36   4.5921e-01   3.9742e-02          90.7 deg
 60   4.5130e-01   4.2231e-02          90.6 deg
 30   4.5130e-01   4.2231e-02          90.6 deg
 66   4.4108e-01   4.0808e-02          90.7 deg

spread -91.6 .. +98.2;  84% of DOF within 10 deg of +/-90
```

The coupled counter-examples (+3.2, −178.4, −3.7, −44.8) hold for a *general* `X`.
This platform's `X` is not general: twelve identical buoys under one plane wave
give a highly structured response, and `A·X` and `B·X` come out nearly parallel.

**So the contradiction does not dissolve on this record.** AD1's reasoning is
sound and worth keeping as a standing caution, but the escape it offered is not
available here — the 90° premise is satisfied to 0.7°.

**AD3 — no collapse.** Broadband, frequency-by-frequency application of `A(ω)`
and `B(ω)`:

```
pointwise, single frequency        |R-T|/|T| = 0.2096    |R|/|T| = 1.0224
broadband, frequency-by-frequency  |R-T|/|T| = 0.2096    |R|/|T| = 1.0221
collapse factor                    1.00x
```

Refuted, and obvious in hindsight: V1 measured the response as **99.92% at the
forcing frequency**. A broadband treatment has nothing to act on when the signal
is monochromatic. The candidate was worth testing and is now closed.

## 2. What actually reduced it: the X3 guard

X3 required reporting **ratio and phase alongside the norm**, on the grounds that
a residual is a difference and `|R-T|/|T|` alone cannot distinguish a missing term
from a rotation from a gain error. Applied here it separates them immediately:

```
|R-T|/|T| = 0.2085     |R|/|T| = 1.0223     arg(R/T) = -11.07 deg
```

A rotation of −11.07° *alone* accounts for 0.1929 of the 0.2085. Rotation plus
the 2.2% gain accounts for 0.1963 — **94% of the residual**.

Fitting **one global complex factor** to all 72 phasors at once — which is exactly
a uniform time shift plus a gain, with no per-DOF freedom:

```
c = 1.0193 * exp(-11.40 deg)      equivalent shift  -0.0995 s = -9.95 dt
residual  0.2058  ->  0.0419      collapse  4.92x
```

**A single two-parameter factor removes 79% of the residual.**

## 3. The remainder lands on AD2's independent bound

What is left after removing the lag is `0.0419`. AD2 bounded the kernel's total
contribution — both channels, worst-case aligned — at **~4.9% of `|mu|`**
(`dA/(A−A_inf) = 0.049`, `dB/B = 0.020`). Those were measured from the kernel
alone, with no simulation and no reference to this residual.

```
residual after removing the coherent lag    0.0419
AD2 kernel bound, measured independently    0.049
```

They agree. So the picture that fits every measurement is: **one coherent lag
plus the kernel's known approximation error**, with nothing left over.

## 4. The leading suspect is this harness, not FloatSim

`-9.95 dt` is suspiciously close to a round **10 steps**, and no integrator
timescale is anywhere near it:

```
one step (mu lagged_unblended)     0.01000 s = 1.15 deg
alpha_f * dt   (rho_inf = 0.8)     0.00444 s = 0.51 deg
alpha_m * dt                       0.00333 s = 0.38 deg
dt/2  (left-endpoint quadrature)   0.00500 s = 0.57 deg
```

The largest is **1.15°** against a measured **11.40°** — an order of magnitude
short. Nothing in the documented conventions produces a ten-step lag.

**That is a reason to suspect the comparison, not the solver.** A clean integer
step count that matches no physical timescale is the signature of an indexing
alignment, and this project has now recorded **five** window/alignment defects —
`mu`'s warm-up, the truncated stored window, the drift magnitude, the panel
reference pose, and a comparison window shorter than the kernel memory. A sixth
would not be surprising; it would be the pattern.

**Do not send this upstream.** The next step is to audit how `recompute_mu`'s
output is aligned against the `xi_dot` samples that produced it, before any claim
is made about FloatSim. If that audit clears, the question becomes real.

## 5. What this changes about the G1.6 investigation

The question has been *"what missing physics is worth 21% of `|mu|`?"* for eight
rounds. The measurement says there is no missing term of that size: there is a
**2-parameter coherent offset** worth 79% of it, and a remainder that matches an
already-measured approximation.

Consistent with AD4 — the mechanisms kept falling because the quantity being
hunted was never the quantity that was there.
