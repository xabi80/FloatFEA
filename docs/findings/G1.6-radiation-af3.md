# G1.6 radiation under AF3 — PASSES at round-off

**Date:** 2026-08-26
**Design:** AF3, pinned before the run (`G1.6-prediction.md` § AH1/AH2)
**Prediction:** round-off. **Result:** `1.359e-15`.

---

## Result

Integrating the exported per-panel radiation pressure over each body's panels,
against the coefficients from the **same BEM solve**, at the case frequency:

```
||R-T||_F / ||T||_F        1.359e-15
||R||_F / ||T||_F          1.000000000
worst single entry         4.815e-15   (buoy4__Heave)
```

Machine precision. The pre-registered target is met.

## The first comparison was 5.9e-4, and that was mine

Comparing instead against `hdb.A`/`hdb.B` **interpolated** to the case frequency:

```
||R-T||_F / ||T||_F        5.914e-04
single global factor       0.999973 * exp(-0.001 deg)
```

`ω = 2.000377` sits between grid points `1.930166` and `2.074677`, at fraction
`0.486` — and `B` varies ~60% across that gap. The panel BEM solved **exactly** at
ω; the reference was linearly interpolated. The difference is **11 orders of
magnitude** above the reconstruction error, so the entire `5.9e-4` is the
interpolation.

> **Third time frequency interpolation has produced a spurious residual** — the
> 8% from the first pass's nearest-neighbour lookup, the AD2 band comparison, and
> now this. **Compare at a solved frequency, or interpolate both sides
> identically.** A reference interpolated across a gap where the quantity varies
> 60% is not a reference.

Had AF3's target been the tabulated `~0.049` rather than round-off, this `5.9e-4`
would have vanished inside it unnoticed — which is AH2's argument for the
round-off target arriving as a demonstration rather than an argument.

## Scope limits — stated, because the scalar does not carry them

**1. Translational rows only: 36 of 72.** `integrate_panel_pressure` returns a
3-component force resultant. The rotational rows need moment arms it does not
provide. **Reporting "G1.6 radiation passes" without this qualifier would be a
gate covering half its rows** — the eighth guard applied before the fact rather
than after.

**2. One frequency.** The panel group holds `ω = 2.0004` only. Storage was not the
constraint (12.2 MB against 985 MB) — the BEM was. A single-frequency panel group
cannot support any multi-frequency reconstruction.

**3. This measures panel reconstruction, which is what AF3 is for.** The
quadrature is common-mode by construction and cancels; the time-domain path
through the convolution is covered by the `mu` replay (bit-identical) and the AG5
fingerprint, not by this number.

## The fingerprint holds alongside it

AH2 requires the per-DOF shape, because under a round-off target a zero no longer
announces a broken comparison. Measured (`G1.6-prediction.md` § re-sync):
heave `0.01×`, surge `10.6×`, pitch `43.5×` on `endpoint/B_tab`, tracking `K(0)`.
A stub cannot produce that shape.

## AK1 — where quadrature is guarded now, and it is NOT here

**A green G1.6 does not cover quadrature. Read this before reading the number.**

Under AF3 the quadrature is common-mode by construction, so it cancels. The direct
consequence, stated plainly:

> **The `k=0` endpoint defect — the one this entire investigation existed to find
> — would now pass G1.6 silently at `1e-15`.**

That is *correct design*, not a hole. G1.6 measures panel reconstruction, and
mixing a quadrature check into it is what produced the permanent `~0.049` residual
AH2 rejected. But the coverage did not vanish; it **moved**, and it moved **into a
different repository**:

| what | where it is guarded now | repo |
|---|---|---|
| panel reconstruction | **G1.6** (this document) | FloatFEA |
| convolution quadrature order | `test_convolution_quadrature_is_second_order` — `3.3 < ratio < 4.7`, and a regression to rectangular shows ~2 | **HSP** |
| the endpoint's physical effect | the permanent pitch/surge radiation-damping gate (`dccbecb`) | **HSP** |

The risk this note exists to prevent is a later reader taking a green G1.6 as
evidence the quadrature is sound. It is not evidence either way. **If HSP's two
gates are ever removed or weakened, nothing in FloatFEA will notice.**

## AK2 — the 36 uncovered rows are where the trouble has always been

The scope limit above is not a completeness chore. Set the covered and uncovered
halves against the measured history:

```
                       endpoint/B_tab      covered by AF3?
  roll                        44.63x            NO
  pitch                       43.51x            NO
  surge                       10.57x            yes
  sway                        10.84x            yes
  heave                        0.01x            yes
```

**The verified half has the mild history; the unverified half is where every
problem has lived.** Roll and pitch — the two DOF carrying a 43–44× historical
excess, the two that drove the whole G1.6 investigation, and the two whose
`K(0)` is four orders above heave's — are exactly the rotational rows
`integrate_panel_pressure` cannot reach.

This does **not** undermine `1.359e-15`. Quadrature is common-mode, so the
endpoint defect would not surface in either half. But it makes the rotational rows
a **priority**, not a tidy-up.

**"Translational rows only, 36 of 72" travels with the scalar wherever it is
quoted** — ninth guard, applied to a coverage fraction rather than a ratio.

## Status

**G1.6 radiation: PASS**, at round-off, on 36 of 72 rows, at one frequency, under
the AF3 design pinned before the run. The remaining 36 rows need a moment-arm
integration and are **not** covered.
