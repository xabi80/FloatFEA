## 2. R541 — the milestone's only element defect

**The shear term's sign in `bending_interpolation` was `+Φξ/6` and should have
been `−Φξ/6`.** Found by the reviewer at the sixty-second verdict; fixed at
`968435a`.

### How the symptom was misread as physics

The defective mass matrix produced free-free and pinned-pinned frequencies that
**rose** under mesh refinement. That contradicts Rayleigh-Ritz, and the response
should have been to suspect the code. Instead a mechanism was constructed for it
— `Φ = 12EI/(κGAL²)` is computed from the element length, so refining the mesh
changes the interpolation and the subspaces are not nested — and the mechanism
was written into a step report, into `F2.md` §141 as a locked plan statement, and
into a closure artifact. **The technical supervisor endorsed it as real physics.**
DL1's pre-registered fallback was then taken on the strength of it.

It was wrong in one character. The field family is `{θ∈P₂, w' = θ − cθ''}` with
`c = EI/(κGA)`, a length-independent constant; `Φ` is only its dimensionless
rendering. The spaces are nested, the rises are `0.000e+00` corrected, and DL0's
one-sided band was achievable all along.

### How it was caught, and by what

Not by more reasoning. By checks that need no reference:

```
cmd    det of the interpolation matrix, L = 4
out    shipped   Phi=0 +6.667e-01  Phi=0.5 +3.333e-01  Phi=1 +0.000e+00  Phi=2 -6.667e-01
       corrected Phi=0 +6.667e-01  Phi=0.5 +1.000e+00  Phi=1 +1.333e+00  Phi=2 +2.000e+00
rule   corrected it is L(1+Phi)/6, the classical shear-flexible denominator;
       shipped it is SINGULAR at Phi = 1 and negative above
out    at Phi = 1 -- L = 1.5945 m, L/D = 2.66, a 277.5 kg member, inside F3's own
       L/D >= 2 limit -- `local_mass` raised LinAlgError on the shipped sign
cmd    the energy identity: q^T k q against the interpolated field's own strain
       energy, eq. 5.36 being that field's energy
out    corrected 1.4e-15 .. 2.0e-15 over Phi = 1e-3 .. 10; shipped 3.8957e-03,
       3.8730e-02, 3.2971e-01, LinAlgError, 3.3041e-01
rule   ROUNDOFF_IDENTITY = 1e-14
out    and the reviewer's fourth witness, sharing none of the module's
       derivation: the classical shear-flexible Hermite shape functions written
       from the (1+Phi) form, matched term by term at 11 stations over seven Phi
       and three spans -- worst 3.6e-15 on N_w, 1.0e-15 on N_theta, all 21
       combinations. THE CORRECTED INTERPOLATION IS THE CLASSICAL ELEMENT.
```

**Why four existing checks were blind, which is the lesson.** The
Euler-Bernoulli checkpoint is at `Φ = 0` by construction. The rigid-body inertias
and `ΦᵀMΦ` are quadratic forms of rigid vectors, whose `c3` is zero — and `c3` is
the only coefficient the shear term multiplies. **The rigid-inertia
parametrisation runs at `Φ = 2.509` and `Φ = 10.169` and passed on the defective
sign.** All 60 rung-2 tests passed on both signs.

**A gate whose checks all live where a coefficient cannot be seen is not a gate
on that coefficient**, and the way to find out is to ask, of each check, which
term it would notice. The energy identity now ships as `rung2`'s answer to that,
with the shipped sign flip as its counter; the reviewer could not make it pass on
a wrong element, and solved its detection threshold at `s − 1 ≈ 4e-9`.

## 6. The lesson, in one line each

**Step 6:** a dropped assertion needs its replacement carrier in the same commit.

**Step 7, and the milestone's:** **an FE convergence result that contradicts the
textbook is a reason to suspect the code, not to find a mechanism for it.** Sixty-two
verdicts of nothing-wrong made a wrong answer easier to believe than a wrong
sign. What broke it was a check with no reference — and the way to have had that
check earlier was to ask which term each existing check would notice.
