## 2. The one real defect, and how it was found

**R625 — the builder accepted a negated element stiffness, including the whole
matrix negated.** `seventh_over_epsilon` sorts `|λ|`, and negating a symmetric
sub-block leaves every rigid motion annihilated — so both shipped halves of G2.1
read their *clean* values on an indefinite element:

```
clean                      neg eig 2  residual 8.7211e-20  7th/eps 9.3791e+11  ACCEPTED
torsion sub-block negated  neg eig 5  residual 8.7211e-20  7th/eps 9.3791e+11  ACCEPTED
axial sub-block negated    neg eig 5  residual 8.7211e-20  7th/eps 9.3791e+11  ACCEPTED
THE WHOLE MATRIX negated   neg eig 7  residual 8.7211e-20  7th/eps 9.3791e+11  ACCEPTED
```

A stiffness that releases energy is not a stiffness, and **nothing in the
repository rejected one** — every spectral read took an absolute value or clipped
at zero, and the only eigenvalue sign test in `floatfea/` was on inertia. Closed by
a signed third clause, `λ_min(k̂)/(‖k̂‖·ε) ≥ -RIGID_MODE_BOUND`, reusing a declared
constant. Clean reads `-2.1425e-03`; the three shapes read `-9.3791e+11` and
`-4.5035e+15`.

**It was found by a reviewer, not by a test**, and the sentence in `rigid.py`
claiming the residual half caught a sign flip was refuted by the cell that found
it. That sentence was mine.

## 3. Two subjects, two ceilings, and why one constant cannot serve both

R624's answer, and the reason F3 declares a tolerance.

| | the gate | the refusal |
|---|---|---|
| subject | the sixteen members of the shipped platform | every deck a reader could write |
| ceiling | `PLATFORM_RIGID_MODE_EXACTNESS = 1.154338e-18` | `RIGID_MODE_EXACTNESS = 1e-15`, unchanged |
| clean worst | `3.528257e-19`, `3.27x` inside | `5.287607e-18`, `51.1x` inside |
| counters | three, each reddening **16 of 16** | **none**, and none is possible |

**The window over the admissible band is empty.** Over 1680 admissible
`(D_o, t/D_o, L)` points the clean worst `5.287607e-18` is already *above* the
weakest counter response `1.571633e-19`, because clean and counter responses move
in opposite directions with slenderness. So no single value both passes every legal
member and is crossed by every counter. A ceiling measured on another quantity may
not host the counters, and the gate's ceiling may not be stretched to a subject on
which no such ceiling exists.

**The gate's window is one decade wide** — `(3.528257e-19, 3.776640e-18)`, `10.70x`
— and that narrowness is a finding rather than a comfort. It is re-derived by the
shipped gate at every run, not re-justified.

## 6. What carries into F4

* **EB6 — the label-provenance gate.** A buoy-label permutation in the **deck
  export** is invisible at `53 passed`, and `buoy_joint_nodes` is how F4 applies
  each gimbal reaction. F4's load-mapping step adds a gate whose expected side is
  each buoy's position read **read-only from FloatSim's own platform definition in
  HSP-stable**, quoting file and line — not from the export — with
  `# expected:` naming that source. The permuted export must redden it. The
  cluster-angle check may be a second, independent expected side.
* **The additive HSP writer.** `res.lam` plus per-body external force and the
  equilibrium reaction, so the joint reactions F1 sized the arms against are
  exported rather than discarded. DX1 measured that the reactions exist in the
  solve and are thrown away: the perturbation residual reads `0.6581 N` against a
  `281.3 N` weight, a ratio of `2.34e-03`.
* **DZ5 — the deck's body inertias are physically inconsistent and the FE keeps
  them**, because inertia-relief equilibrium with FloatSim's loads requires it.
  Every body's `J_G` satisfies the lamina identity exactly, and the remainder the
  split leaves is PSD but violates the triangle inequality at every `f > 0`. The
  slack is **not** linear in `f` on the platform — `slack/f` spans `-5.3539e+08` to
  `-2.9819e+08`, a `1.795x` spread — and is exactly linear on the four hubs. For
  Xabier, on FloatSim, not on the FE.
* **`admissible()` tests PSD and not realisability, by decision (EA3)**, because
  requiring realisability would force `f = 0` and leave the arms massless.
* **R638 --- `RIGID_MODE_EXACTNESS` is not guarded at the strength the gate's
  ceiling is.** A `REGISTERED` row for it, with cells reaching
  `floatfea.model.platform`'s namespace, and a declared size for the `1.0e-8`
  injection. Section 4a has the measurement: `3784x` of silent headroom.
* **The counter registration's reach.** All three counters redden 16/16 at the
  declared injection, and the binding edge is `rotational_block` at
  `3.088842e-15`, cleared by `3.24x`. If a section or a length on this platform
  moves far enough to cross that, the counter-case moves.
