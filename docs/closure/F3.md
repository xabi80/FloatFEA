## 2. The one real defect, and how it was found

**R625 — the builder accepted a negated element stiffness, including the whole
matrix negated.** `seventh_over_epsilon` sorts `|λ|`, and negating a symmetric
sub-block leaves every rigid motion annihilated — so both shipped halves of G2.1
read their *clean* values on an indefinite element:

```
clean                      neg eig 2  residual 8.7211e-20  7th/eps 9.3791e+11  ACCEPTED
torsion sub-block negated  neg eig 5  residual 8.7211e-20  7th/eps 9.3791e+11  ACCEPTED
axial sub-block negated    neg eig 5  residual 8.7211e-20  7th/eps 9.3791e+11  ACCEPTED
THE WHOLE MATRIX negated   neg eig 7  residual 8.7211e-20  7th/eps 9.3791e+11  ACCEPTED
```

A stiffness that releases energy is not a stiffness, and **nothing in the
repository rejected one** — every spectral read took an absolute value or clipped
at zero, and the only eigenvalue sign test in `floatfea/` was on inertia. Closed by
a signed third clause, `λ_min(k̂)/(‖k̂‖·ε) ≥ -RIGID_MODE_BOUND`, reusing a declared
constant. Clean reads `-2.1425e-03`; the three shapes read `-9.3791e+11` and
`-4.5035e+15`.

**It was found by a reviewer, not by a test**, and the sentence in `rigid.py`
claiming the residual half caught a sign flip was refuted by the cell that found
it. That sentence was mine.

`(D_o, t/D_o, L)` points the clean worst `5.287607e-18` is already *above* the
weakest counter response `1.571633e-19`, because clean and counter responses move
in opposite directions with slenderness. So no single value both passes every legal
member and is crossed by every counter. A ceiling measured on another quantity may
not host the counters, and the gate's ceiling may not be stretched to a subject on
which no such ceiling exists.

**The gate's window is one decade wide** — `(3.528257e-19, 3.776640e-18)`, `10.70x`
— and that narrowness is a finding rather than a comfort. It is re-derived by the
shipped gate at every run, not re-justified.
