## 3. The two blocking items verdict 116 carried, and how each was repaired

Both are recorded in full in the commit message of `d8554a9`. Both were found by the
reviewer **inverting an attack the invocation asked for**, which is the arrangement
working rather than a lucky round.

### R755 — the quantifier was wider than the sweep that measured it

Three sites said `F6_API_STRESS_PLAUSIBLE_MIN` is "a floor beneath EVERY admissible
configuration". It is a floor beneath every admissible `(F_y, D/t)`. **`e` is the clause's
third argument, nothing in the module refuses one, and the floor breaks below
`e = 5.000000e+10 Pa`** — closed form `floor × 300 / 0.6`, and the module's own bisection
agrees to `0.000e+00`. `E_STEEL` clears it by `4.2000×`; aluminium at `7.0e10` is still
admitted and a 30 GPa modulus is refused.

`D` was never the risk: `F_xc` is a function of the ratio, so the gate's `D = 2.5` costs
nothing, which the reviewer verified over five diameters. **`e` is the same defect one
level out** — a figure correct on the axes its author chose to sweep, which is R694's shape
for the third time in this project.

Repaired at the three sites — `floatfea/tolerances.py`, `floatfea/checks/api_wsd.py`'s
docstring, and `docs/milestones/F6.md:241` — each now stating the scope actually swept and
naming `e` as unguarded. `scripts/measure/r754_stress_vs_grade.py` gained TABLE 4, which
measures the `e` boundary both ways, so the figure is regenerated rather than typed.
**The exposure itself is C96 and is NOT closed** (§ 5 below); all three sites say so.

### R756 — the locked plan's regeneration gate was asserted nowhere

`docs/milestones/F6.md:261` locks it: *"the table regenerates identically from stored
results (G6.3's shape), and the top-ten list is stable under a re-run."* Nothing under
`tests/` read `results/F6/` at all, and the deliverable **was not deterministic**: eight
lines of 457 differed between two runs, every one a pytest wall-clock timing pasted into
§ 5's status block. The counts are what each row claims and they are deterministic; the
duration was never part of the claim.

Repaired: the generator publishes the counts and strips the duration, and
`tests/regression/test_f6_deliverable_agrees_with_itself.py` gained the plan's gate — two
runs byte-compared, plus a regeneration-with-gates that reads the output for a timing.

**AND THE FIRST VERSION OF THAT GATE COULD NOT FAIL**, which is the part worth carrying
forward. Two tests, written to close a finding about an unasserted gate, gave `12 passed`
with the defect restored: the determinism test passes `--no-gates` so no timing is
produced, and the wall-clock test read only the shipped file, which had already been
regenerated clean. The repaired gate gives `1 failed, 11 passed` on R756's own state. **A
gate written to close a finding about a missing gate is exactly where a vacuous assertion
hides**, because the attention is on the finding.

A third defect fell out of writing it: the generator could not be pointed outside the tree
at all — six `relative_to(ROOT)` calls raised on a scratch `--out`. A generator that cannot
write to a scratch path cannot be checked for determinism, which is part of why this gate

## 4. The numbers that moved, and why

**`F6_API_STRESS_PLAUSIBLE_MIN = 1.0e8` is new** (R754). It is not a widening of the grade
range, which is untouched at `[2.0e8, 1.0e9]`: it is a **second predicate**, because a
reduced stress is not a grade. `allowable_axial_compression` substitutes § 3.2.2(b)'s
`F_xc` for `F_y`, and with the grade predicate in that path the module refused `21357` of
`177006` grid points — `12.066%` of its own declared six-grade sweep — on values it had
computed itself. After the repair, `0` of `177006`.

The binding corner is the **grade floor at the `D/t` ceiling**, `1.3655759329e+08 Pa`,
which no single-grade literal reaches: my own denying figure for C72 was taken at S355 and
was correct there. Above `D/t ≈ 252.5` at S690 the elastic term caps `F_xc` at `4.2e8 Pa`
independent of the grade, which is why the minimum sits at the floor and not at any grade a
reader would think to try — and why a **constant** is a legitimate repair here where
`C_m`'s resolution needed a function: `F_xc` is bounded below on the admissible domain with
the bound *attained*, where `C_m`'s infimum is `0`, attained.

**No golden file moved. No published member force or utilisation changed** across either
blocking repair: F6 ships S355 at `D/t = 13.8889`, where `F_xc = F_y` and nothing is
reduced by local buckling at all.

## 6. The limitations every F6 result carries

These are on the face of both deliverables and are repeated here because a closure artifact
is where someone looks first.

1. **The section is a stand-in.** A stiffness equivalent in `EI` and `EA` for a
   triangulated truss of undecided depth. It does not represent that truss's local member
   forces, its joints or its buckling modes. **The governing finding is about the
   stand-in**, so replacing it replaces the finding.
2. **The masses are placeholders.** The `f` split, the body masses and the platform inertia
   assumption are declared inputs. `f` is the single largest lever in the calculation: the
   static root utilisation spans `0.8104` to `1.6208` across the ladder, **linearly**, and
   the shipped rung is *not* the worst. § 7's `K` and `C_m` moves are both under `0.035`.
   Until the masses are real the utilisations are a ranking, not a magnitude.
3. **The hub arms' assumed line mass does not fit inside the assumed section** — 15 t/m
   against a realisable 10.299 t/m at steel density. Recorded rather than adjusted, because
   adjusting it would mean inventing a section.
4. **One heading, 0°.** Three of the six force components — `N`, `Vy`, `Mz` — are
   identically zero on all 48 static rows, so whatever they carry is entirely a function of
   the single heading that was run. A heading sweep is the only thing that can exercise
   them.
5. **`T = 10 s` is excluded from the governing basis** because it exceeds the deep-water
   breaking steepness at `H = 24.2 m`. It is also the largest of the six on the per-case
   stress bound, so excluding it is the expensive direction.
6. **API RP 2A-WSD is written for fixed jackets.** Neither it nor ISO 19902 is a
   certification basis for a floating platform. It is used because it is the simplest
   defensible member check, and the label says so on every output.
7. **The one-third increase is not applied**, because these cases carry no declared return
   period. Six regular waves cannot support a design statement.
8. **§ 3.2.2(b)'s elastic local-buckling term is inert at S355** — it first governs from
   `D/t ≈ 252.5` at S690 — so half that clause is exercised only by the gate, never by a
   shipped result.

## 7. What this milestone cost, honestly

Seven reviewed rounds across two steps. **Every blocking finding in the last three rounds
was in prose, apparatus or a published figure — not in the element and not in the
clauses.** R753 was a published clause that was false as an identity; R754 a grade
predicate in front of a reduced stress; R755 a quantifier wider than its sweep; R756 a
non-deterministic deliverable against a gate the plan already named. The clause arithmetic
itself, once hand-checked at two points either side of every branch boundary, moved once
(R741/R742) and has not moved since.

**Three things that paid, measured rather than asserted:**

- **The continuum grid over a decision rule's parameter space**, which found R755 by
  running the model at a configuration no diff pointed at. EG4(e) had paused corpus
  batches; FD1 now records this as the substitute, because in the round that mattered it
  produced both blocking findings where a batch would have been the spend.
- **The regeneration check on a published deliverable**, which found R756.
- **Generating the deliverable instead of writing it.** Four published sentences in this
  milestone were false and each was a figure a reader could not regenerate. The generator
  caught a fifth while it was being written — "four orders" where the two constants are
  `7.9` decades apart — in the one part of the document still written by hand.

**Two things that did not pay**, and both are recorded so the next milestone does not
repeat them: six consecutive rounds of true-but-gateless figure findings on step 1, whose
own coverage measurement fell from 2-of-22 to 1-of-19 unseen shapes while they ran; and two
rounds spent trying to *detect* source-tree prose claims with keyword lists, which caught
0 of 20 and 3 of 8 against unseen phrasings. The detectors were deleted; the prohibition
and the reviewer's reading are what remain.

**And the mechanism found its own blind spot twice in the last round.** C88 shows that 21%
of revision 3 was invisible to three guards because one generated-marker comment sat near
the foot of a section; C89 shows a guard that cannot distinguish a truthful "no run" from a
forged empty table. Neither is a defect in the element. Both are reasons a green suite is
evidence and not proof, which is the sentence this project was built around.
