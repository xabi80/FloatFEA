## 6. The limitations every F6 result carries

These are on the face of both deliverables and are repeated here because a closure artifact
is where someone looks first.

1. **The section is a stand-in.** A stiffness equivalent in `EI` and `EA` for a
   triangulated truss of undecided depth. It does not represent that truss's local member
   forces, its joints or its buckling modes. **The governing finding is about the
   stand-in**, so replacing it replaces the finding.
2. **The masses are placeholders.** The `f` split, the body masses and the platform inertia
   assumption are declared inputs. `f` is the single largest lever in the calculation: the
   static root utilisation spans `0.8104` to `1.6208` across the ladder, **linearly**, and
   the shipped rung is *not* the worst. § 7's `K` and `C_m` moves are both under `0.035`.
   Until the masses are real the utilisations are a ranking, not a magnitude.
3. **The hub arms' assumed line mass does not fit inside the assumed section** — 15 t/m
   against a realisable 10.299 t/m at steel density. Recorded rather than adjusted, because
   adjusting it would mean inventing a section.
4. **One heading, 0°.** Three of the six force components — `N`, `Vy`, `Mz` — are
   identically zero on all 48 static rows, so whatever they carry is entirely a function of
   the single heading that was run. A heading sweep is the only thing that can exercise
   them.
5. **`T = 10 s` is excluded from the governing basis** because it exceeds the deep-water
   breaking steepness at `H = 24.2 m`. It is also the largest of the six on the per-case
   stress bound, so excluding it is the expensive direction.
6. **API RP 2A-WSD is written for fixed jackets.** Neither it nor ISO 19902 is a
   certification basis for a floating platform. It is used because it is the simplest
   defensible member check, and the label says so on every output.
7. **The one-third increase is not applied**, because these cases carry no declared return
   period. Six regular waves cannot support a design statement.
8. **§ 3.2.2(b)'s elastic local-buckling term is inert at S355** — it first governs from
   `D/t ≈ 252.5` at S690 — so half that clause is exercised only by the gate, never by a
   shipped result.
