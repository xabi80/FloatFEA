### What the gate actually asserts, in one paragraph

The exact displacement field for each constant-strain state is imposed on the
assembled stiffness, **without a solve**, and the residual is read at the
interior nodes only. Rotational degrees of freedom are scaled by a characteristic
length so the residual is dimensionally homogeneous, and it is normalised by the
largest entry of the scaled matrix and the largest scaled displacement. The
result is compared with `PATCH_TEST_EXACTNESS`. Nothing in the quantity depends
on the conditioning of a solve, which is what the earlier formulation did and why
it was replaced at the ninth verdict.

## The margin, and the operating point it sits on

`PATCH_TEST_EXACTNESS = 5e-15` holds, and the margin between the ceiling and the
worst clean corpus configuration is the **smallest it has ever been**:

| round | worst clean ratio | margin |
|---|---|---|
| through 156 entries | `0.0887x` | `11.3x` |
| 163 entries | `0.0982x` | `10.2x` |

| 177 entries | `0.1261x` | `7.93x` |
| **187 entries** | **`0.2765x`** | **`3.62x`** |

Four consecutive rounds, four rises, each from a region the previous round had
not entered. The trend is monotone and it has not yet flattened.

**The operating point, measured at `2f92c58` on the entry that sets the figure**
(`ck_cleanmax_D18p8_tw0p499_aniso1e6`):

```
D            18.7706 m          t            9.3647 m        t/D    0.49890
I_y / I_z    1.000e+06          member L/D   6.736           L/r_min  26.9
```

**Two constraints bind, and neither is `BEAM_ADMISSION_L_OVER_D`.** The
anisotropy sits **exactly** at `1e6`, the top of the corpus's declared range, and
the wall sits at `0.4989 D` against the strictly-less-than-`D/2` the section
constructor enforces. That corner is why four rounds of log-uniform sampling
walked past it: sampling reaches a corner with probability about zero.

`member L/D` is **6.736**, more than three times the locked `BEAM_ADMISSION_L_OVER_D = 2.0`,
and no entry in the top eight is nearer than `2.899`. So the figure **does not**
currently sit on the admission limit and does not have to be re-solved if that
limit moves for a reason of its own. What would require re-solving it is a change
to either constraint that does bind — the anisotropy cap or the wall bound — or
any search that reaches further into that corner. Recorded here because the
inference that the margin rests on `L/D >= 2` is the natural one to draw from
"it sits on the admissible boundary", and the geometry does not support it.

**This is a statement about corpus coverage, not about the element.** The field
error itself did not move; the shipped assertion holds at every entry; the
published figure was true of the corpus it described at every commit.

## Process findings worth keeping

**A guard on a guard is the same kind of object as the thing it guards.** Three
counter-cases in this step shipped asserting *themselves* rather than the gate,
and the meta-test written to catch that could not catch two of them. It now
carries two cells — the counter must run its gate, and be sized by the constant
it defends — with three defective bodies shipped as negative controls, each
asserted to be rejected by its own cell and admitted by the other. Delete either
cell and a control turns green.

**A number typed where a name would do goes stale silently.** This species
appeared in five consecutive rounds, in tolerance comments, in step reports, in
commit messages, and finally in the locked plan, where a *solved boundary* had
gone wrong by 8% in the unsafe direction. The answer is mechanical: figures are
generated, referenced by name, and the file is checked against a fresh run.

**A withdrawal recorded and enforced by nothing survives one round.** A sentence
the plan explicitly withdrew was re-introduced into the code's justification a
day later, with a word added that made it impossible rather than merely
unmeasured.

**A closing condition that names sites is closed site by site.** Five consecutive
rounds closed a site-naming condition at some of its sites. The guard is now a
test at **line** resolution, comparing the verdict's own coordinates against the
old side of `git diff -U0`.

**The reviewer's adversarial corpus is what moved every figure in this step.**
Not one of the 187 entries was written by the implementer.
